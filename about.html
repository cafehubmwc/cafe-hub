Our Story

Welcome to Cafe Hub

Tucked right opposite Dine Time Restaurant, Cafe Hub started as a small juice counter and grew into an all-day hangout for anyone craving something fresh, fast and genuinely good. Every juice is cold-pressed to order and every plate leaves the kitchen hot.

From thick milkshakes and falooda to crispy fried chicken and our loaded pizza uttapam, we keep the menu wide and the ingredients honest — so there's always something new to try on your next visit.

Why Cafe Hub

Juices, Shakes & Best Bites in Town!

We started with a single blender and a promise: nothing pre-mixed, nothing sitting around. That's still how we run the counter today — cold-pressed juices squeezed to order, shakes blended thick, and every plate cooked fresh the moment you ask for it.

Cafe Hub Story

Every Cup Has a Story — Our Task is to Delight You

What began as a small counter serving cold-pressed juices to the Mahendra World City crowd has grown into a full menu of shakes, falooda, kunafa and hearty plates. The idea never changed: keep it fresh, keep it fast, and keep the ingredients honest.

Today Cafe Hub is where regulars stop for a quick juice on the way to work and where friends linger over a shared platter late into the night — open every day until 2 AM.
